Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

AI Shorts Bot ("we", "our", or "the service") is a creator tool designed to help users manage AI-generated short-form video content and connect their workflow with TikTok's official Content Posting API.

When you connect your TikTok account, the service may process information made available through TikTok's authorization and API, such as your TikTok account identifier, authorization information, and information required to publish content.

2. Video Content

The service may process video files and related content information that you choose to upload or publish through the service.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide and operate the requested creator features, including connecting to TikTok and supporting video publishing workflows.

Our use of information obtained through TikTok APIs is limited to the purposes necessary to provide the requested functionality and is subject to applicable TikTok developer requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to TikTok when you explicitly use functionality that requires communication with TikTok's services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the service. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, comply with applicable obligations, or maintain the service.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using the service or disconnect its access to your TikTok account through the applicable TikTok account and authorization controls.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact method provided by the AI Shorts Bot service operator.
